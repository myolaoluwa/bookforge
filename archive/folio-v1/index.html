Good questions.
Clear answers.

Does my manuscript need to be finished?+

We can discuss your project at any stage. For final formatting, your manuscript should be edited and proofread so that later text changes do not disrupt the layout.

Can you help with print and ebooks?+

Yes. The proposed service covers print interiors and EPUB editions. Your chosen publishing platform, trim size, images, and layout needs determine the final scope.

What if I only need formatting?+

You can choose formatting on its own. Cover design and publishing guidance are separate services, so your project can focus on exactly what you need.

How much will my book cost?+

A quote depends on word count, images, tables, design complexity, and the formats you need. Start with the project brief so those details can shape a clear scope before work begins.

Will I get to review the design?+

The proposed process starts with a sample layout or design direction. Review stages and revision rounds should be agreed in your project scope before production begins.

Do you guarantee sales or publication?+

No. Design and formatting help prepare your book, but sales, retailer approval, distribution, and publication decisions remain with the relevant platforms and the author.
