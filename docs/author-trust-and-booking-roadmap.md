# Bookforge: author trust and booking roadmap

Researched 4 November 2026 using public provider pages, compared with the current Bookforge implementation. These are observations and recommendations, not endorsements or verified conversion results. Private portals, payments and service quality were not tested. No website functionality changed during this research.

## Comparable providers

| Provider and evidence | Observed approach | Application to Bookforge |
|---|---|---|
| [Damonza](https://damonza.com/formatting/) | Separate print, ebook and combined offerings; starting prices, inclusions, extras and access to cover/interior examples. | Show the work beside an explicit service scope. |
| [Ebook Launch](https://ebooklaunch.com/print-book-formatting/) | Word-count price bands, complexity caveats, timing, formatter communication and quote/invoice stages. | Publish approved budget guidance and explain what changes the quote. |
| [Ebook Launch quote form](https://ebooklaunch.com/formatting-quote-form/) | Staged format/platform questions and an explanation of quote acceptance, payment and delivery. | Improve our existing staged inquiry; keep initial contact lighter than production intake. |
| [Miblart FAQ](https://miblart.com/faq/) | Creative brief requirements, named designers/managers, revision and payment explanations, and a client portal link. Policies vary by service. | Explain who works on a book, who answers the author and how feedback works. |
| [BookBaby design](https://www.bookbaby.com/book-design) | Book examples, a cover quote configurator, visible contact options and manuscript-stage/genre questions. | Pair relevant proof with a clear route to a human. |
| [BookBaby proofing guidance](https://support.bookbaby.com/hc/en-us/articles/206284897-How-does-BookBaby-s-eBook-proofing-process-work) | Explains approval and distinguishes author text changes from provider mistakes. | Explain review responsibilities and corrections before booking. |
| [Reedsy designers](https://reedsy.com/design/book-designer) — marketplace benchmark | Specialist profiles, portfolios, author reviews, genre matching, quotes and rights guidance. | Make the person and relevant experience verifiable. |

Provider promises belong to those businesses. Do not copy their guarantees, payment terms or credentials into Bookforge.

## Current gaps

Bookforge already has service descriptions, process content, portfolio/filter/detail infrastructure, team and testimonial routes, Goodreads scopes and a staged proposal form. Approved client work, real biographies and endorsements have not been supplied. Inquiry delivery still needs real configuration. An admin CMS is not an author project portal.

## Priority 0: foundations before accepting projects

| Addition | Author experience | Dependency / acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| Working inquiry and receipt | Submit once, receive a reference and understand the next step. | Configure the database, spam verification, sender and inbox. Test saving, notifications, receipts, duplicates and failures. Never claim delivery that did not happen. |
| Real studio identity | Know who runs the business, who will work on the book, their relevant experience, timezone and monitored contact channel. | Owner-approved biographies, portraits, roles and business details. Verify every credential. |
| Useful work samples | Open readable interiors and covers; understand exactly what Bookforge contributed. | Rights-cleared client work or clearly labeled original demonstrations. Include genre, format, brief, decisions and deliverables. Public book links can corroborate publication, but not prove our contribution by themselves. |
| Clear service scope | Know what arrives, what the author supplies, review rounds, timing assumptions, exclusions and who handles publication. | Extend existing service descriptions with owner-approved operational terms. Distinguish editing from formatting. |
| Cost guidance | Judge budget fit before writing a detailed brief. | Actual starting prices or ranges, currency, qualifying scope and complexity factors. No invented rates or unconfigured instant estimates. |

## Priority 1: make the decision and booking easier

1. **Open the book.** Add a sample viewer for cover, print spread and ebook preview, with zoom and brief design annotations. Place it near the existing craft study. Keep essential evidence ungated. Use approved material; label demonstrations. A mockup does not establish EPUB accessibility or technical compliance.
2. **Relevant testimonials.** Connect an approved quote to an author, book and service. Link to an original review/author page where available. Place matching proof on service pages. Avoid invented ratings or unsupported sales claims.
3. **Optional consultation.** Keep the quote as the primary action; offer a secondary conversation for uncertain authors. Use a real calendar with timezone, availability, preparation guidance and cancellation/rescheduling. Publish only a sustainable response-time commitment.
4. **A more relevant brief.** Add conditional format/platform questions to the current form. Capture stage, genre, approximate word count, target date and help required. Include “I’m not sure.” Avoid an account or full-manuscript requirement for first contact. Make phone optional unless essential.
5. **Review and correction clarity.** Explain sample approval, agreed revision rounds, provider mistakes, author rewrites, additional formats and post-delivery support. Choose a bounded commitment the studio can honor. Avoid unconditional retailer acceptance guarantees.
6. **Rights and confidentiality.** Explain manuscript ownership, design usage rights, stock/font licenses, source-file inclusion, access, retention and portfolio permission. State the actual AI/subcontractor policy. These statements must match operations and contracts; obtain appropriate policy review.
7. **Sample proposal and handoff.** Publish a labeled example showing scope, formats, milestones, responsibilities, review allowance, exclusions, payment schedule and final checklist. This explains the relationship; it is not a fabricated client contract.
8. **Practical FAQ.** Answer budget, timing, rights, editing versus formatting, changes after approval, platform issues, payment and what happens after inquiry. Put relevant answers close to each service’s CTA.

Suggested primary CTA: “Get a tailored quote.” Supporting copy: “Tell us about your book. Review the scope before you commit.” Confirm any claims such as free consultation or response within a stated period before publishing them.

## Priority 2: delivery features after the foundations work

| Feature | Purpose | Dependency |
|---|---|---|
| Private author workspace | Milestones, proof versions, consolidated feedback, approval history and final files. | Recurring delivery volume, tested access controls and retention procedures. |
| Private manuscript intake | Collect samples/full files after initial fit and consent. | Private storage, permitted file types/sizes, access expiry, malware handling and deletion procedures. No public manuscript bucket. |
| Proposal acceptance and invoicing | Present agreed scope, record acceptance and collect agreed payments through a supported provider. | Approved pricing, currencies, payment/cancellation terms and tax handling. |
| Guided service finder | Suggest relevant help from stage and format needs. | Stable service boundaries; allow uncertainty and human review. |
| Resource library | Manuscript preparation, proof review, trim sizes and EPUB/print explanations. | Actual expertise and editorial review. Prefer a few useful guides to generic content volume. |
| Conversion measurement | Quote starts/completions, consultation bookings, qualified inquiries, proposal acceptance and signed projects. | Privacy choices and a baseline. Do not send manuscript text or personal project details to analytics. |

## Recommended journey

Home: cinematic introduction and explicit service statement → selected work/sample viewer → scope and budget guidance → people and relevant author proof → process and approval checkpoints → FAQ → tailored quote.

Service page: relevant example → inclusions/exclusions → pricing factors and timing → revision/support terms → matching proof → preselected inquiry.

Booking: lightweight brief → genuine confirmation → human scope discussion/optional call → written proposal → acceptance and agreed payment → private production intake → sample/proof approval → handoff.

Preserve the original film, deliberate sound activation and reduced-motion support. Authors must be able to pause, mute, read the offer and reach the quote immediately.

## Owner inputs

Approved identities and bios; rights-cleared samples; genuine testimonial consent; actual scope, currency, prices, capacity, response and support terms; business inbox/calendar/payment choices; manuscript access/retention and artwork/AI policies. Do not fill these gaps with plausible invented claims.

## First release recommendation

Activate inquiry delivery, publish real people and samples, add scope/budget guidance, explain review/ownership and connect an optional consultation route. Build portal/payment automation later. This sequence addresses the observed gaps; any conversion benefit is a hypothesis to measure.
