1. Strategic positioning
Bookforge combines publishing production with discoverability strategy. The website is both a premium creative agency presence and a usable lead-generation system. Each visitor must quickly understand (1) what Bookforge does, (2) whether the team has done comparable work, and (3) how to request a project proposal.

Positioning statement: Bookforge Publishing Studio helps independent authors refine manuscripts, create publication-ready book interiors and covers, prepare files for self-publishing, and build a more credible author presence online.

Brand line: Beautiful books are made with intention.
Suggested campaign line: From manuscript to a book ready for readers.
Homepage search heading: Professional Book Editing, Formatting & Cover Design for Independent Authors.
Primary CTA: Request a Publishing Proposal.
Secondary CTA: Explore Our Work.
Other context-specific CTAs: Discuss Your Book; Request a Cover Design Quote; Plan My Goodreads Setup.

Conversion design: Clear CTA above the fold; contextual CTAs at the end of each service; project-driven CTAs on portfolio details; straightforward inquiry steps and expectation setting. Never hide essential navigation inside animation.

Differentiation to demonstrate, not merely claim: Named specialists and responsibilities; specific formatting/cover deliverables; visible authentic samples; explained quality checks; custom project scope; practical Goodreads deliverables; clear proposal and revision process.

Success metrics: Qualified inquiries, inquiry completion rate, inquiry-to-consultation rate, qualified lead-to-client rate, traffic to service pages, organic impressions/clicks, indexed pages, successful portfolio engagement, median site performance. Capture baselines after launch, then establish targets rather than inventing them.
2. Brand identity / design direction
Concept: The Modern Editorial Atelier. Combine premium publishing aesthetics with cinematic art direction. Use restrained motion, tactile paper/ink imagery, sophisticated high contrast, and photographic book presentation. Avoid overused gold-on-black luxury templates and 3D extravagance at the cost of usability.

Colors: Midnight Ink #111B1D; Ivory Paper #F4F0E8; Antique Copper #BE9670; Sage Slate #63746C; deep charcoal #283032; primary text ink-on-ivory and ivory-on-ink. Verify accessible text contrast before implementation. Copper is decorative or used on high-contrast surfaces only, not automatically small body text.

Fonts: Cormorant Garamond (headlines and typographic storytelling) + Manrope (body/UI). Self-host where licensing permits; subset and preload critical weights; use system fallbacks. Do not use fonts or photographs without confirmed rights.

Brand system: Distinct wordmark BOOKFORGE, optional stylized BF/bound-book monogram; horizontal, stacked, favicon, dark/light versions; cover-grid and spine motifs; paper grain used sparingly; editorial folios/counters; an alternating dark/light page rhythm.

Recommended visual inspiration: Full-screen sculptural book object, cinematic spotlighting on authentic cover assets, editorial magazine page layouts, bold negative space, beautiful macroscopic print details. Design original work; do not copy other sites.

Photography: Real portraits of Adele, Rachel, and Ava, photographed/edited consistently with natural lighting. Preserve actual appearance. Real covers and interiors are primary visual assets. Temporary mockups can be visually labeled in staging but cannot be presented as completed client work.

Motion direction:
- Hero: single beautiful book object derived from licensed/owned portfolio assets; subtle pointer parallax on desktop, low-motion fallback on touch, no large autoplay video prerequisite.
- Reveal: staggered headlines and book images with transforms/opacity; content remains present in HTML, visible without JavaScript when feasible.
- Scroll: gentle pinned editorial sequences in one or two carefully chosen sections; no forced scroll hijacking.
- Projects: responsive cover gallery, thoughtful hover depth, smooth transitions; direct URLs and keyboard navigation preserved.
- Details: small page-section reveals, before/after interior comparison where real proof exists, book rotation only when device capability allows.
- Accessibility: respect prefers-reduced-motion; no motion-required access to information; avoid vestibular triggers, flashing, or long loaders; visible focus states and skip links.
- Performance: lazy-load below-fold motion, media and WebGL; defer GSAP modules; use static/mobile alternatives; stop animations when out of viewport; no animation as blocker to LCP.

After submit: server-side validation and spam protections; idempotent write to DB; send branded notification to agency email; send reassuring receipt to author (no delivery or deadline promise unless approved); show success state with inquiry reference; record audit event; enforce retry and delivery status to avoid duplicate sends. If email provider is unavailable, persist inquiry and surface retryable notification state. Do not expose inquiry data in public routes or analytics.

Proposal lifecycle: New → Reviewing → Needs Information → Proposal Sent → Won / Closed / Archived. Add internal notes and optional assignment to Adele/Rachel/Ava. Templates are editable and not automatically sent without intended action; no bulk marketing.

Security/consent: contact-only consent is distinct from marketing subscription. Privacy notice, lawful processing basis, data retention, deletion request handling, signed upload expiry, storage quotas, abuse/rate limits, malware controls where applicable, bot protection such as Turnstile or equivalent, careful attachment handling, sanitization, CSRF defenses as appropriate, email header injection protections. Avoid storing passwords or platform credentials.

Email: recommend Resend or Brevo transactional via API; domain/SPF/DKIM/DMARC verified before launch; agency inbox such as hello@bookforgepublishing.ink only after provisioned and tested. Use a verified sender and record delivery attempts without exposing private mail contents.

Gate: Every route has correct visible H1, purposeful body, functioning CTA, unique title/canonical, usable 360px mobile viewport; no fake reviews or projects.

Phase 6 — Cinematic refinement
Build: GSAP effect components, hero book visual, on-scroll editorial reveals, selective depth/pinning, project transitions, texture and art direction, responsive touch fallback, prefers-reduced-motion implementation, media budgets and feature flag to disable heavy motion.

Phase 7 — QA, security, SEO and production launch
Build/test: E2E, integration, accessibility, RLS negative tests, performance throttling, cross-browser/mobile matrix, robots/sitemap inspection, structured data validation, error-monitoring, backup restore rehearsal, admin and delivery runbooks, deployment and DNS, Search Console and analytics verification.
Gate: No P0/P1 bugs, no unauthenticated leakage, no broken primary CTA, approved assets and bios, successful form + email end-to-end, public indexed pages render real content in initial HTML, noindex for admin/staging/drafts, HTTPS enabled, canonical origin verified.
Phase 8 — After launch improvements
Focus: monthly Search Console query/page audit, refine headings based on observed intents, A/B test message/CTA only with enough data, grow original portfolio and articles, improve conversion funnel, carefully add analytics dashboard/CRM integrations only after V1 is stable.

Do not skip phases or claim complete without evidence. Build in vertical slices and run checks after each phase. Prioritize a functioning site and authenticated CMS before visual flourishes.
12. Testing and launch acceptance matrix
Functional: route coverage, navigation, service package preselection, responsive menu, inquiry submit/validation/receipt, admin content CRUD, draft/publish/archival, redirects, email error states, media delete and cache invalidation.

Security: owner/editor/viewer roles, RLS for published/unpublished, private file signed URLs, admin route auth, secret scanning, output escaping/XSS checks in rich text, form spam, content upload type and size controls, rate limiting, audit logs, restore from backup, no database service credentials on client.

Accessibility: automated axe rules plus keyboard/manual screen reader tests; skip links; focus visibility and sequence; semantic headings/landmarks; alt descriptions for meaningful covers; captions for any videos; minimum contrast; touch target sizes; zoom 200%; reduced motion.

SEO: direct fetch source includes headings and meaningful page content; exact canonical and titles; valid metadata; site map excludes drafts; no duplicate service intent, correct 404, working internal links, OG previews, image dimensions, descriptive alt, validated structured data, HTML-first links.

Performance: 360–390px Android, mid-range laptop, throttled mobile network, and recent desktop; route-specific Lighthouse profiles and real-user Core Web Vitals; media and animation budgets; optimized fonts/images; CLS prevention; monitor cold-start/dynamic route response.

Content: written proof source recorded; actual asset and review permissions; no placeholder marketing statistics; person bios real; all service descriptions reflect confirmed delivery capability; domain contact verified; legal copy finalized by business/legal owner.

- Confirm the exact legal/trading business name and public contact email.

14. Explicit V1 exclusions
No direct checkout or price cards; no fake Goodreads account integration or promise of badge approval; no artificial client stats; no paid/incentivized reviews; no mass messaging; no storing author login credentials; no full client portal; no complex 3D engine by default; no auto-generated SEO article farm; no automatic profile claiming on third-party sites; no unverified locations, awards, staff credentials or book publication outcomes.
15. Production definition of done
The public website looks distinctive and coherent; is usable and accessible with and without animations; presents approved real services/team/portfolio; has differentiated SEO pages and crawlable HTML; has the dedicated Goodreads conversion page with accurate terms; supports working proposal form plus real mail delivery; lets Adele manage content and leads without a developer; keeps drafts and author data secure; runs automated and manual QA; uses the verified bookforgepublishing.ink canonical domain; and has monitoring, backups and a maintenance runbook.
